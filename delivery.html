Delivery & Return Policy

Last updated: September 2026.

Delivery Charges

  • Inside Dhaka: ৳70
  • Outside Dhaka: ৳130
  • Free delivery on orders above ৳3,000

Exact delivery charges are confirmed on WhatsApp once we know your delivery area, and may vary slightly for remote locations.

Delivery Timeline

Orders are typically dispatched within 1–2 business days of confirmation. Delivery inside Dhaka usually takes 1–3 days; outside Dhaka, 3–6 days, depending on the courier partner and your location.

Cash on Delivery

All orders are currently Cash on Delivery. You pay the courier when your parcel arrives — please inspect the package before accepting.

Exchange Policy

We accept exchanges within 3 days of delivery if the item is unworn, unwashed, and in its original packaging with tags attached. Message us on WhatsApp with your order details to start an exchange.

Return Policy

Returns are accepted only for items that arrive damaged, defective, or different from what was ordered. Please share photos of the issue over WhatsApp within 24 hours of delivery so we can arrange a resolution.

What's Not Covered

  • Change of mind after wearing or washing the item
  • Minor color variation between screen and fabric
  • Items without original tags or packaging
