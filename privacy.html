Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026. This is placeholder policy text — please have it reviewed against Bangladesh's applicable regulations before launch.

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order or contact us, we may collect your name, phone number, delivery address, and any details you share with us over WhatsApp, our contact form, or email.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use this information to confirm and deliver your order, respond to questions, and — only if you subscribe — send occasional updates about new arrivals and offers.

3. Cart Data

Items you add to your cart are stored locally in your browser (localStorage) and are not sent to our servers until you choose to place an order via WhatsApp.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share order details with delivery partners as needed to fulfill your order.

5. Your Choices

  • You can ask us to delete your contact details at any time by messaging us on WhatsApp.
  • You can unsubscribe from marketing messages at any time.

6. Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions, please reach out via WhatsApp or the Contact page.
